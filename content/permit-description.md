# The request, in the applicant's words

> I am transferring the land to be situated around my house to avoid the recurrence of a
> nuisance that the walkway created before I bought the building and was able to close it
> off. It was used frequently by people cutting through, leaving trash, condoms, used
> needles — it was known back then as "Little Ivanhoe."

**In plain terms:** application BPS26-0517 asks to move about 525 square feet between two
adjoining lots on Ivanhoe Avenue — a boundary adjustment, with no construction — so the old
cut-through walkway is absorbed into the applicant's own parcel.
